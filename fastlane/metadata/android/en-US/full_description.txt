Picture Trans turns your phone into a local file server so you can move
files between your Android device and your computer over the same Wi-Fi —
no USB cable, no cloud, no app to install on the computer.

How it works:
- The app runs a built-in HTTP server and shows a URL plus a QR code
- Open that URL in any modern browser on your computer
- Phone to PC: browse and download photos, videos and Download-folder
  files, individually or multi-selected
- PC to Phone: drag and drop to upload — images and videos land in your
  gallery, everything else in Downloads
- Video preview with seeking (HTTP Range), thumbnail grid, live transfer
  progress and history on the phone

Privacy and security:
- No ads, no trackers, no telemetry, no accounts. Nothing is uploaded
  anywhere except your own local network.
- A random 16-character token is generated per install and embedded in
  the URL path (/t/<token>/); requests without it get a 404.
- The server binds to the LAN interface only. Traffic is plain HTTP,
  a deliberate trade-off to avoid certificate management on a home
  network — do not port-forward or expose the server to the internet.
- Media access uses Android's scoped storage (MediaStore); the app never
  asks for broad filesystem permissions on Android 10 and later.
- Backup is disabled so the access token cannot leak through adb backup.

Requirements: Android 8.0 (API 26) or later. Phone and computer must be on
the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot.
